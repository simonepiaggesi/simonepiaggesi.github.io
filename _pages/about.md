I am Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Pisa and a member of the Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining Laboratory (KDD Lab), a joint research initiative of the ISTI Institute of CNR, the Department of Computer Science of the University of Pisa, and Scuola Normale Superiore. 

I work on explainable and trustworthy AI. Currently, my focus is on:

* **AI for Science** ---  interpretable methods for biomedical research (e.g., cancer drug response prediction), supporting AI-assisted scientific discovery 
* **Tabular Deep Learning** --- post-hoc explanation methods, differentiable decision trees, hypernetworks, tabular foundation models
* **Graph Machine Learning** --- mechanistic explainability for node embeddings and GNNs (link prediction, epidemic forecasting)

I received my Ph.D. in Data Science and Computation (2023) from Alma Mater Studiorum University of Bologna, based at ISI
Foundation in Turin. I hold an M.S. in Physics of Complex Systems (2017) and B.Sc. in Physics (2014) from the University of Turin.
